Frequently Asked Questions

What is LMI?
Lenders Mortgage Insurance protects the lender (not you) if you default on your loan. It's usually required when borrowing more than 80% of the property value.
Is this estimate accurate?
It's an approximation — actual LMI premiums vary by lender, insurer (Helia/QBE), loan type and state stamp duty on the LMI premium.
Is this free?
Yes — completely free, no sign-up needed.
How do I avoid LMI?
Save a 20% deposit, use a guarantor, or check eligibility for the First Home Guarantee scheme which allows eligible buyers to borrow up to 95% with no LMI.
